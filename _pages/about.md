# Wenbo Xiong

**Ph.D. Student · Fudan University**  
Shanghai Innovation Institute · Shanghai, China

## About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in **Intelligent Science and Technology** at **Fudan University**, affiliated with the **Shanghai Innovation Institute**. I received my Master's degree in Control Science and Engineering (2024) and my Bachelor's degree in Automation (2021), both from **Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications**.

My research interests include **generative modeling, reinforcement learning, optimal control**, and **AI for scientific applications**.

## Research Interests

- Generative Models and Diffusion Models
- Reinforcement Learning and Optimal Control
- AI for Science
