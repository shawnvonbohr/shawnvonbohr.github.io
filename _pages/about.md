## About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in the 2024 cohort at the **School of Data Science, Fudan University**, majoring in **Intelligent Science and Technology** and jointly trained at the **Shanghai Innovation Institute**. My doctoral advisor is **Prof. Libo Wu** at Fudan University, and I also receive research guidance from **Ke Wei** at Fudan University.

I received my **M.S. in Control Science and Engineering** in 2024 and my **Bachelor's degree in Automation** in 2021, both from **Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications**.

My research interests include **AI for Science**, particularly in **climate and energy**, as well as **reinforcement learning, optimal control, and game theory**.
